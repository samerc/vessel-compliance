# Features Recap

1. **Dashboard**: Unified view of vessels.
2. **Fleets**: Grouping and management.
3. **Documents**: Drag-and-drop linking.
4. **Reports**: Compliance-focused exports.

## How to use
- Click **Vessels** or **Fleets** to manage data.
- Click **Details** to manage documents.
- Use **Excel/PDF** buttons for reporting.
