## Quotation Builder is Here

We are excited to introduce the **Quotation Builder** — a comprehensive tool to create, manage, and export professional insurance quotations directly from the app.

### What You Can Do

**Build Quotations**
- Create P&I, Hull & Machinery, War Risk, FD&D, Loss of Hire, and Cargo quotations
- Tabbed editor with sections for Insured, Vessel, Conditions, Trading, Warranties, Deductibles, Exclusions, Sanctions, Subjectivities, Premium, and more
- Multi-vessel quotations with per-vessel premium breakdown
- Rich text editing for all text sections

**P&I Quotations**
- Select from admin-managed clause presets (Full Cover, Restricted Cover, etc.)
- Add warranties from sets or individually, with custom warranties and bulk import
- Deductibles with sub-limits, text deductibles, and exclusions
- Limits of liability with configurable sub-limits
- Additional clauses with vessel scope

**Hull & Machinery Quotations**
- Agreed value with template texts
- Hull clause selection with configurable conditions
- Multiple clause alternatives — each with its own conditions and premium
- Increased Value (IV) clause support

**Alternatives (P&I & Hull)**
- Create multiple alternatives per quotation to present different coverage options
- Each alternative has its own conditions, warranties, deductibles, exclusions, and premium
- Exports clearly separate alternative-specific items from shared items

**Premium Management**
- Per-vessel and per-alternative premium inputs
- NCB (No Claims Bonus) and UPCC (Upfront Continuity) sequential discounts
- Instalment schedules with configurable defaults
- Non-refundable premium options
- Payable premium calculated per alternative

**Revisions & Duplication**
- Create revisions of sent quotations — previous versions are locked, new revision is editable
- Duplicate any quotation to quickly create a similar one for another vessel
- Full revision history with version tracking

**Export**
- Export to PDF and Word (DOCX) with professional formatting
- Configurable section ordering per quotation type
- All sections rendered with proper tables, bullet lists, and formatting

**Quotation Settings (Admin)**
- Manage clause presets, warranty sets, deductible templates, exclusions, and subjectivities
- Configure section text defaults and trading warranty templates
- Quotation type management with custom codes
- Section order configuration per type
- Instalment defaults
